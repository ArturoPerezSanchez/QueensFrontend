# MiniChess asset attribution

The chess piece artwork is the Cburnett set, generated through
[`python-chess`](https://python-chess.readthedocs.io/) and used under its
BSD/GPL/GFDL licensing terms. The original artwork is by Colin M.L. Burnett.

Additional selectable sets are distributed through the
[Lichess piece collection](https://github.com/lichess-org/lila/tree/master/public/piece).
The bundled set directories contain their own attribution and license details:

| Set | Creator | License |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Celtic | Maurizio Monge | MIT |
| Chessnut | Alexis Luengas | Apache-2.0 |
| Fantasy | Maurizio Monge | MIT |
| Firi | James Faure | CC BY 4.0 |
| Kiwen Suwi | neverRare | CC BY 4.0 |
| RhosGFX | RhosGFX | CC0 1.0 |
| Spatial | Maurizio Monge | MIT |

Standard puzzle positions are a quality-filtered subset of the CC0
[Lichess open puzzle database](https://database.lichess.org/#puzzles).

Gardner 5x5 positions are generated and validated offline with
[Fairy-Stockfish](https://github.com/fairy-stockfish/Fairy-Stockfish), which is
distributed under GPL-3.0. The engine is build tooling and is not included in
the web application.
